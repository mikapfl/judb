**A layout that follows the window.** The Source pane now keeps at least 80
columns of code — PEP 8's line length, measured in your editor font — whatever
the window does: on a narrow window the console folds *under* the source instead
of fighting it for width. The lower panes reflow to match, from one row across
to two and then three as space runs out, and the toolbar shrinks to its icons
rather than pushing the app sideways. Any lower pane can be closed with the ×
in its header when you want the room; **▤ Panes** in the toolbar shows what is
hidden and brings it back, and your choice is remembered for next time.
